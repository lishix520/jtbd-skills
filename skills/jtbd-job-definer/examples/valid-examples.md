> **Note**: The valid statements below represent **illustrative candidate job hypotheses** extracted from scenario evidence. In actual engagements, candidate jobs must be empirically validated with job executors.

## Domain 1: Medical & Healthcare

### Example 1.1
- **Candidate Statement**: `Restore blood flow in a blocked artery`
- **Job Executor**: Interventional Cardiologist
- **Context**: During an angioplasty procedure
- **Rationale**: Uses `Verb (Restore) + Object (blood flow in a blocked artery)`. Completely solution-free (does not mention balloon catheters or stents) and outcome-free (does not say "quickly" or "safely").

### Example 1.2
- **Candidate Statement**: `Treat a chronic wound`
- **Job Executor**: Wound Care Nurse
- **Rationale**: Stable functional objective. Solution-free (does not mention specific bandages, ointments, or therapies) and emotionally neutral.

### Example 1.3
- **Candidate Statement**: `Monitor patient vital signs during surgery`
- **Job Executor**: Anesthesiologist
- **Rationale**: Describes the underlying goal of looking at display monitors during surgery without referencing the display monitor technology.

## Domain 2: Software & Knowledge Work

### Example 2.1
- **Candidate Statement**: `Align team members on project goals`
- **Job Executor**: Project Lead / Engineering Manager
- **Rationale**: Solution-free (replaces "Use Slack", "Send emails", or "Hold Zoom calls"). Outcome-free (does not say "seamlessly" or "quickly").

### Example 2.2
- **Candidate Statement**: `Prepare a project status update for cross-functional review`
- **Job Executor**: Program Manager
- **Context**: `for cross-functional review` (Contextual clarifier distinguishes this from internal personal notes).
- **Rationale**: Focuses on functional progress. Replaces "Create a PowerPoint deck" or "Build a Jira dashboard".

### Example 2.3
- **Candidate Statement**: `Locate digital evidence for a legal proceeding`
- **Job Executor**: Legal Discovery Specialist / Litigation Attorney
- **Rationale**: Replaces specific forensic software or manual file searching tools.

## Domain 3: Consumer Products & Lifestyle

### Example 3.1
- **Candidate Statement**: `Prepare a hot beverage for consumption`
- **Job Executor**: Consumer / Homeowner
- **Rationale**: Broad enough to encompass tea, coffee, hot chocolate; solution-free (replaces stovetop kettle, Keurig, espresso machine).

### Example 3.2
- **Candidate Statement**: `Listen to music while on the go`
- **Job Executor**: Commuter / Music Listener
- **Context**: `while on the go` (distinguishes mobile listening from home theater or studio recording context).
- **Rationale**: Solution-free (replaces iPod, smartphone app, Walkman, headphones).

### Example 3.3
- **Candidate Statement**: `Obtain breakfast while commuting to work`
- **Job Executor**: Morning Commuter
- **Context**: `while commuting to work`
- **Rationale**: Replaces "Buy a McDonald's milkshake" or "Drive through Dunkin' Donuts". Focuses on the actual functional job.

## Domain 4: Hardware, Trades & Industrial

### Example 4.1
- **Candidate Statement**: `Cut wood`
- **Job Executor**: Carpenter / Tradesperson
- **Rationale**: Completely solution-free (replaces "Use a Bosch circular saw") and outcome-free. "In a straight line" is a performance criterion belonging in a Desired Outcome statement (*Minimize the deviation from the intended cutting line*).

### Example 4.2
- **Candidate Statement**: `Control indoor temperature in a residence`
- **Job Executor**: Homeowner
- **Rationale**: Solution-free (replaces "Adjust Nest thermostat", "Turn on AC").

### Example 4.3
- **Candidate Statement**: `Prevent weeds from impacting crop yields`
- **Job Executor**: Corn Farmer / Agricultural Producer
- **Rationale**: Customer perspective ("prevent weeds from impacting crop yields") rather than vendor perspective ("kill weeds with herbicide X").

## Summary Checklist for Valid Examples:
1. `Verb + Object + [Optional Clarifier]` syntax.
2. 0% mention of products, features, apps, or technologies.
3. 0% mention of speed, cost, ease, accuracy, or safety.
4. 0% mention of confidence, pride, status, or feelings.
5. Framed from the person performing the job.
