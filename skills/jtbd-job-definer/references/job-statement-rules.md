# Core Functional Job Statement Rules & Guidelines

This reference document provides authoritative rules for drafting, auditing, and rewriting **Core Functional Job Statements** in Outcome-Driven Innovation (ODI).

---

## 1. Grammatical & Syntactic Structure

A Core Functional Job Statement must strictly follow this structure:

$$\text{Core Functional Job} = \text{Verb} + \text{Object of the Verb} + [\text{Optional Contextual Clarifier}]$$

### Components:

1. **Verb**:
   - An active functional verb describing the action being performed (e.g., *Restore*, *Prepare*, *Listen to*, *Prevent*, *Monitor*, *Formulate*, *Maintain*).
   - Must be from the job executor's perspective, NOT the vendor's.

2. **Object of the Verb**:
   - The noun or noun phrase representing what is being acted upon (e.g., *blood flow*, *a hot beverage*, *music*, *weeds*, *crop yields*).
   - Must NOT name a product, technology, feature, or tool.

3. **Optional Contextual Clarifier**:
   - Added ONLY when necessary to distinguish the job from a different job in a different situation.
   - Example: `Listen to music [while on the go]` vs. `Listen to music [in a recording studio]`.
   - **WARNING**: Do NOT use the contextual clarifier to sneak in solutions (e.g., `using an AI tool`), performance outcomes (e.g., `in less than 5 minutes`), or emotional states (e.g., `without stress`).

---

## 2. The Solution-Free Rule

A Core Functional Job MUST be completely independent of any past, present, or future solutions.

### Why?
Solutions change over time (e.g., vinyl records -> cassette tapes -> CDs -> MP3s -> streaming services), but the Core Functional Job (*Listen to music*) remains stable for decades or centuries.

### Blacklisted Solution Terms (Reject if present):
- Products, Brands, Platforms: *Slack, iPhone, Excel, Salesforce, Zoom, Tesla, Keurig, Nest*
- Technologies / Mediums: *AI, LLM, Machine Learning, Blockchain, Cloud, App, API, Database, PDF, Website, Script*
- Channels / Formats: *Online, In-person, Email, SMS, Dashboard, Paper, Form*

### Transformation Matrix:
| Invalid (Solution-Contaminated) | Valid (Solution-Free Core Job) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Use Slack to align the team* | **Align team members on project goals** |
| *Build an AI dashboard to track sales* | **Monitor sales performance** |
| *Boil water with a stovetop kettle* | **Prepare a hot beverage for consumption** |
| *Use circular saw to cut wood* | **Cut wood** |

---

## 3. The Outcome-Free Rule

A Core Functional Job describes **WHAT** functional progress is being made, NOT **HOW WELL**, **HOW FAST**, or **HOW SAFELY** it is executed.

### Why?
Metrics of speed, cost, precision, and safety are **Desired Outcomes** (which are captured separately per step in the job map). Mixing outcomes into the job definition makes it impossible to quantify where customers are underserved.

### Blacklisted Outcome Words (Reject if present):
- Speed / Time: *quickly, fast, rapidly, instantly, in real-time, promptly*
- Accuracy / Quality: *accurately, error-free, precisely, perfectly, flawlessly, correctly*
- Ease / Effort: *easily, effortlessly, simply, seamlessly, conveniently*
- Safety / Risk: *safely, securely, reliably, without risk, safely*
- Financial / Cost: *cheaply, cost-effectively, affordably, inexpensively*

### Transformation Matrix:
| Invalid (Outcome-Contaminated) | Valid (Outcome-Free Core Job) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Quickly and accurately cut wood in a straight line* | **Cut wood in a straight line** |
| *Safely restore blood flow in a blocked artery* | **Restore blood flow in a blocked artery** |
| *Easily book a flight online* | **Book travel accommodations** |

---

## 4. The Emotion-Free & Social-Free Rule

A Core Functional Job is strictly functional. Emotional and social aspirations must be separated into Emotional Job and Social Job classifications.

### Blacklisted Emotional / Social Words:
- Emotional: *confidently, calmly, happily, proudly, without anxiety/stress, fearlessly*
- Social: *professionally, like an expert, to impress others, respectfully*

### Transformation Matrix:
| Invalid (Emotion/Social-Contaminated) | Functional Job | Emotional/Social Job (Separate) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Confidently present project status to leadership* | **Present project status for executive review** | *Emotional: Feel confident during high-stakes presentations* |
| *Look like a good parent by giving kids a treat* | **Provide afternoon refreshments to children** | *Social: Be perceived as a caring parent* |

---

## 5. Scope Boundaries: Neither Too Broad nor Too Narrow

Finding the right level of abstraction is critical for actionable insights.

### Rule of Thumb:
- **Too Broad**: Contains multiple independent jobs that require different capabilities, vendors, or life domains (e.g., *Manage a business*, *Live a healthy life*).
- **Too Narrow**: Describes a single step within a job, a feature interaction, or a specific product use (e.g., *Boil water*, *Click a button*, *Open an app*).

### Scoping Guide Table:

| Level | Example Statement | Evaluation | Resolution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Too Broad** | *Manage home comfort* | Too broad. Spans HVAC, furniture, lighting, security. | Focus on: *Control indoor temperature* |
| **Just Right** | **Prepare a hot beverage for consumption** | **Valid Core Functional Job**. Stable, meaningful, solution-free. | Use as Core Job |
| **Too Narrow** | *Boil water* | Too narrow. Boiling water is just a process step in preparing a beverage. | Expand to: *Prepare a hot beverage for consumption* |

---

## 6. Executor Perspective vs. Vendor / Lifecycle Activity

A Core Functional Job describes what the **customer (Job Executor)** is trying to achieve, NOT what the vendor or buyer is doing.

### Distinctions:
1. **Core Functional Job**: *Restore blood flow in a blocked artery* (Surgeon)
2. **Purchase Decision / Buying Activity**: *Evaluate surgical equipment options* (Hospital OR Manager)
3. **Consumption Chain Job**: *Clean and sterilize the surgical tool* (Sterilization Tech)
4. **Vendor Internal Activity**: *Market angioplasty catheters to hospitals* (Vendor Sales)

Do NOT confuse consumption chain tasks (install, setup, clean, repair, dispose) or purchasing tasks with the Core Functional Job itself.
