# JTBD Switch Interview Guide Rules & Principles

## Purpose

The **Switch Interview Guide** implements Bob Moesta's Timeline & Switch Interviewing methodology.

It acts as an interactive conversational coach for everyday users, product managers, marketers, and founders. It transforms customer statements into actionable, non-leading interview questions while maintaining structured evidence tracking for underlying methodology skills.

---

## The 6 Decision Timeline Phases

Every customer switch progresses through 6 distinct chronological phases:

1. **First Thought**: The initial event, friction, or realization that the current situation is no longer ideal.
   * *Goal*: Identify the specific moment in time when the thought first occurred.
2. **Passive Looking**: Noticing alternatives in the background without active effort or budget.
   * *Goal*: Uncover what information entered the customer's awareness.
3. **Active Looking**: Spending dedicated time, energy, or money to evaluate solutions.
   * *Goal*: Identify specific triggers that turned passive interest into active search.
4. **Decision / Big Hire**: Making the trade-off, securing budget, and choosing a specific solution.
   * *Goal*: Uncover the final trade-off decision and anxiety resolution.
5. **First Use / Onboarding**: Setting up, installing, and first attempting the new approach.
   * *Goal*: Measure whether the solution matched expectations or created immediate friction.
6. **Ongoing Use / Little Hire**: Repeated usage, renewal, habit formation, or eventual abandonment.
   * *Goal*: Check if the solution continues to deliver progress over time.

## 🛑 Cardinal Rules of Switch Interviewing

### Rule 1: Never Discuss Features When the Customer Asks for Features
- ❌ **Incorrect (Leading)**: Customer says "I want a Jira button for reports." -> Interviewer asks "What fields should the Jira button have?"
- ✅ **Correct (Event Probe)**: "Tell me about the last time you needed that report—what was happening, and how did you generate it?"

### Rule 2: Anchor Every Question in a Specific Past Event
- ❌ **Incorrect (Speculative)**: "Would you pay $20/month for an automated reporting tool?"
- ✅ **Correct (Behavioral)**: "When you looked for a reporting tool last month, what solutions did you actually try, and what did they cost?"

### Rule 3: Single Primary Question Focus
- Do NOT output a 15-question script. Provide **ONE primary question** to speak out loud, accompanied by a single follow-up probe and one explicit forbidden question.

## Question Formulation Cheat Sheet

| Situation / Customer Fragment | Recommended Next Question Type | Example Question to Ask | What NOT to Ask |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **No Input (Starting)** | Event Anchor | "When was the last time you tried to solve this problem, and what were you doing right before?" | "What is your dream product for this?" |
| **User Complains About Tool** | Push Trigger | "What specifically happened the last time that tool failed or fell short?" | "Why don't you switch to a better tool?" |
| **User Requests a Feature** | Current Workaround | "How are you currently handling that step today without that feature?" | "How would you like that feature to work?" |
| **User Mentions New Tool** | Active Looking Trigger | "What was happening on the day you decided to actually search for that tool?" | "What do you like most about that tool?" |
| **User Mentions Hesitation** | Anxiety Probe | "What were you most worried about right before you made the final decision?" | "Don't you think the benefits outweigh the risks?" |
